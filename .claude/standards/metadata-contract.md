# STD-METADATA — Metadata Contract

Canonical metadata SHALL include title, subtitle, contributors and roles, language, audience band, description, short description, keywords, subjects/categories, edition, publication date, rights statement, identifiers, formats, trim dimensions, page count, and accessibility features.

Platform metadata is derived from canonical metadata. Platform-specific truncation or category mapping SHALL not alter the canonical record. Do not use deceptive keywords, competitor names, misleading superlatives, fake endorsements, or claims unsupported by the product.

For puzzle and activity products, canonical metadata SHALL also record: product family (book_type), puzzle types included, difficulty range, solution placement, and whether the interior is colour or black-and-white. For coloring products, record the art style and whether all art is original. For digital printables, record page size (US Letter / A4) and minimum DPI.

KDP-specific fields (BISAC, age range, grade range) and Etsy/Gumroad tags are platform derivatives maintained separately and re-validated against current platform rules before each submission.
