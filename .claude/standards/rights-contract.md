# STD-RIGHTS — Rights Ledger Contract

Create one rights record for every coloring line-art file, font, clip element, puzzle word list, reference image, generated asset, illustration, photograph, and trademark reference used in a release.

Required evidence includes creator/provider, source, acquisition date, creation method, license or contract, commercial-use status, formats, territories, term, attribution, modification rights, consent where relevant, disclosure obligations, evidence paths/hashes, and clearance status.

`unknown`, `restricted`, `expired`, and `escalated` records block release when included in the package.

Coloring line art is **original-only**: no tracing, no derivative of protected images, no imitation of a living artist's style by name. Every coloring-art asset SHALL have a corresponding originality attestation record. Generated puzzle grids and word lists derived from public-domain dictionaries or purpose-built word lists SHALL have their source and license recorded. Font licenses SHALL explicitly permit embedding in the target formats (print PDF, digital PDF). Do not assert ownership or copyrightability of AI-generated material without legal review.
