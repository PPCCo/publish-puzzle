# STD-RESEARCH — Asset Source and Originality Contract

This framework uses **asset source records** and **originality attestation records** in place of general-purpose research source and claim records.

## Asset source records

An asset source record captures the provenance of non-original inputs: word lists, reference images, font files, clip elements, public-domain illustrations, or any third-party material incorporated into a product. Each record SHALL capture: asset ID, title or description, provider or author, source type, acquisition date, retrieval URL or identifier, license or contract reference, commercial-use confirmation, and archived evidence path.

Any asset whose license, origin, or commercial-use status is `unknown` or unresolvable SHALL be replaced before release.

## Originality attestation records

Every coloring line-art file and every custom illustration SHALL have an originality attestation record stating: the asset ID and hash, the creator identity, the creation method and tools, an explicit declaration that the work is not traced from or substantially derivative of a protected original, that no living artist's style was imitated by name, and the attestation date. Confidence below 0.9 triggers escalation.

Puzzle grids, journal prompts, and text generated by code do not require an originality attestation but do require an asset source record for any word list or template they consume.
