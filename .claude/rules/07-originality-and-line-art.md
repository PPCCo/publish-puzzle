# Originality and line-art

- All coloring line-art and interior illustrations SHALL be original works: no tracing of third-party images, no derivative use of protected images, and no imitation of a living artist by name or recognisable style.
- Every illustration asset MUST carry an originality attestation recorded in the rights ledger before it may advance past the `interior` gate; attestation records the creator, creation method, tool/model version, and a statement that no protected reference was traced or closely derived.
- Do not imitate a living artist by name or use an identifiable person's likeness without documented authority.
- Do not reproduce or closely adapt characters, logos, or designs that are subject to active trademark or copyright protection.
- Public-domain reference images MAY be used as loose compositional inspiration but SHALL NOT be traced or reproduced; record the reference source and confirm its public-domain status.
- Provide meaningful alt text or a documented decorative designation for every visual asset.
