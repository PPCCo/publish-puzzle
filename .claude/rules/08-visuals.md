# Visual assets

- Follow the approved visual bible and illustration brief.
- Record creator/provider, model/tool, prompt or commission brief, seed/version where available, edits, license, and commercial-use evidence.
- Verify character continuity, labels, cultural representation, dimensions, resolution, and colour/contrast against the approved brief and colour policy.
- Do not imitate a living artist by name or use an identifiable person without documented authority.
- Provide alt text or a documented decorative designation for every visual asset.
