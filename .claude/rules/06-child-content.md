# Child and tween content

- Calibrate vocabulary, sentence length, emotional intensity, visual density, puzzle difficulty, and concept load to the approved age band: `kids-4-8`, `kids-6-10`, or `tween-8-12`.
- **kids-4-8** — imagery SHALL be friendly and familiar; no scary creatures, dark themes, or ambiguous threat; cell sizes and type SHALL be large enough for fine-motor limitations; puzzle difficulty SHALL be forgiving with clear visual scaffolding.
- **kids-6-10** — modest difficulty progression is permitted; imagery remains positive and age-appropriate; avoid violent, sexualised, or disturbing themes in both line-art and puzzle content.
- **tween-8-12** — challenge level may rise but SHALL not cross into horror, graphic violence, or age-inappropriate social themes; content remains suitable for school and family settings.
- Avoid condescension, manipulative urgency, unsafe imitation, sexualisation, graphic violence, and undisclosed commercial persuasion across all child age bands.
- Present activities involving scissors, heat, or other physical hazards with explicit supervision requirements and age-appropriate safety guidance.
- Escalate medical, self-harm, abuse, grooming, or child-protection concerns immediately without proceeding.
