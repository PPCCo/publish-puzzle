# Metadata and discoverability

- Maintain one canonical metadata record and derive channel variants (kdp, etsy, gumroad, generic).
- Titles and descriptions must accurately represent the product (puzzle-book, activity-book, coloring-book, guided-journal, workbook, planner, or digital-printable) and the intended audience band.
- Keywords must be relevant, non-deceptive, and non-infringing.
- Do not insert competitor creator names, fake awards, unverifiable bestseller claims, or concealed age-inappropriate terms.
- Preserve contributor role, edition accuracy, and product-family classification.
