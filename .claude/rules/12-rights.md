# Rights

- Every release asset must map to a rights-ledger record.
- `unknown`, `restricted`, `expired`, and `escalated` included assets block release.
- Public-domain claims require jurisdiction, term analysis, source, and evidence.
- Open licenses require exact version, attribution, derivative/share-alike conditions, and compatibility analysis.
- Legal review identifies issues and evidence; it does not replace qualified counsel.
